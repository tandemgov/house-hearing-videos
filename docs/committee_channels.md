## Committees Without YouTube Channels

Some committees and most subcommittees do not have their own YouTube channels. Subcommittee hearings are matched against the parent committee's channel.

## Notes

- "Clips only" channels post opening statements, highlights, or floor speeches rather than full hearing recordings
- "Legacy only" channels have historical content but no recent uploads from the 118th Congress
- Match rates are qualitative assessments based on 118th Congress results
- Channel IDs use the `UC` prefix; uploads playlists use `UU` (swap the prefix)
